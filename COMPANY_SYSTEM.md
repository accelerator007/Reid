# Reid company system

Reid combines people, projects, research, CRM, tasks/requests, workshops/forms, documents, notifications, scoped AI agents and the company WhatsApp inbox. The current workspace is bilingual and permission-filtered. Leave requests exist in Work; payroll is not a claimed active module. Operational UI intentionally de-emphasizes money while preserving historical finance records.

The connected-operations update links a customer conversation to a contact/lead, request, task and optional project, with assignment notifications and concrete Home actions. See [implementation/release status](docs/CONNECTED_OPERATIONS.md); source availability is not deployment evidence.

Working hours come from validated timesheets/activity, not attendance clock-in. Customer contact names and WhatsApp group names never grant company authority. Current architecture, rules and integration blockers are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md); old product states are preserved under `docs/history/`.
