Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and collect as little data as possible — only what's needed to answer your inquiry. Last updated: July 2026.

Who we are

Reid is a technology company based in the Sultanate of Oman, specialized in software product development and technical consulting. This policy applies to our website reidpro.com and any communication made through it.

What data we collect

  • Contact form data: your name, email, and message text — which you provide voluntarily when you reach out.
  • Anonymous technical data: aggregated visit statistics (visits, pages, device type) with no personal identity.
  • We do not use advertising/tracking cookies, and we do not build personal profiles of visitors.

How we use your data

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide the requested service or information.
  • To communicate with you regarding your project or request.
  • To improve the website and understand which sections interest visitors (via aggregate statistics).

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, and we do not use it for marketing without your permission.

Third parties we rely on

  • Form processing (FormSubmit): contact-form content is sent via the FormSubmit service to deliver it to our inbox. Your data is subject to that service's privacy policy during transmission.
  • Hosting (Cloudflare): the site is hosted on Cloudflare's network, which may process basic technical data (such as IP address) for security and performance.
  • WhatsApp: when you contact us via the WhatsApp button, WhatsApp's privacy policy applies to your conversation.

Data retention

We keep contact messages for as long as needed to respond to you and manage our relationship, then delete them when no longer needed. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you, correct it, or request its deletion. To exercise any of these, email us at contact.us@reidpro.com and we'll respond promptly.
