# Permissions
Roles are additive: Owner, Super Admin, Admin, HR, Sales, Employee, Project Member, Research Member, Guest. Owner alone approves L4. HR approves hiring L3. Sales accesses CRM. External members see only assigned projects/research. Signed file access is checked at file level.

Account lifecycle changes are restricted to active Owner/Super Admin callers through `manage-account`; Admin may inspect the governance center and audit trail but cannot mutate accounts. Every role or status change requires a recorded reason. The current user cannot suspend or change their own roles, Owner accounts are protected from suspension/removal, and only an Owner may grant or modify Super Admin access. Direct client writes to account controls and roles are not permitted. Reid's two designated Owner identities are Ali (`alialajmi524@gmail.com`) and Sheikha (`sheikhaalmamari4@gmail.com`).

Owner and Super Admin manage WhatsApp administrator bindings. A linked user must already hold Owner, Super Admin or Admin; linking a phone never grants an application role. An administrator can read only their own binding and cannot enable, disable or relink themselves. The QR webhook rechecks both active account status and current roles for every message. Non-Owner administrators cannot use WhatsApp to reach HR or Finance agents unless they also hold the matching application role.

Direct QR conversations from an enabled active linked administrator start in assistant-active mode. This affects reply automation only and does not expand the administrator's application roles. A manual response from the governed Inbox pauses that conversation until an Owner enables it again.

Unknown direct-message senders receive only the bounded customer assistant: no company memory, internal tools or administrator actions. An explicit request for a human pauses that single chat and emails Ali and Sheikha once; an Owner can later re-enable automation from the Inbox. Routine customer or administrator messages do not generate Owner email notifications.

The QR Owner group is narrower than ordinary administrator chat: only an enabled active Owner mapped to Ali or Sheikha may invoke it, and only inside the exact enabled group JID registered as `Reid_Owner`. Invocation requires standalone `ريد`/`ريّد`/`Reid`, an actual bot mention, or replying to a message authored by Reid. Registering the group grants no new role or agent permission, and Owner/Super Admin may disable the registration from Connections.

The `/owner` brief and its aggregated company snapshot are restricted to Owner and Super Admin. Admin cannot call the snapshot function. It combines OMR-only commercial totals, operational counts, approvals, applications, task/project risk and bounded receivable/project lists; it grants no mutation permission and does not change any underlying row-level policy.

Business cases are visible to active Owner, Super Admin, Admin and Sales users. Sales can start a case from a non-lost CRM deal and advance only to an issued quote. Owner, Super Admin or Admin approval is required to accept the quote as a contract, mark the CRM deal won, and create the private delivery project. Only Owner or Super Admin can issue the invoice, record customer payments, reverse a payment, or close a collected engagement. The database rejects skipped stages and requires a written reason for every stage transition.

Owner, Super Admin and Admin can cancel a pre-invoice engagement; after invoicing, cancellation is Owner/Super Admin only. Cancellation is refused until every posted payment is reversed. A permitted cancellation voids an unpaid invoice, archives its delivery project, and preserves the reason in the lifecycle history. This prevents “cancelled” work from leaving an active receivable or active project behind.

Financial documents and payment rows are visible only to Owner and Super Admin. Customer receipts are written only through the governed payment functions: partial payments update the outstanding balance, exact full payment marks both invoice and case collected, and reversal requires a reason and reopens the balance. Direct browser writes cannot change `paid_amount` or mark an invoice paid. The register records money received externally; it is not permission to initiate a bank transfer.

Sales may issue a business-case quote with validated line items, discount, document-level tax rate and validity date, but Sales cannot read the private finance register or Reid's legal/bank profile. Owner and Super Admin alone edit that company profile, create standalone finance drafts, and issue an invoice. A business invoice copies the approved quote economics; the database calculates all totals and freezes the seller, customer, lines, totals, dates and terms when a document becomes issued. The browser print/PDF view reads that frozen copy. Starting delivery also creates one kickoff milestone and one planning task owned by the manager who approved the start.

Active company users can read the employee directory and departments. Employees can complete their own onboarding items, update assigned task state, create their own private calendar entries, upload/read their own documents, and record their own timesheets. They can read only their own KPIs and performance reviews. Owner, Super Admin, Admin, and HR can manage employee records, onboarding, task assignment, company calendar entries, announcements, documents, KPIs, and reviews. Storage policies repeat the employee/HR/Admin ownership boundary at the object layer.

Projects are visible only to Admin, the assigned project manager, and project members. Admin creates projects; the manager or a member explicitly labeled `manager`/`lead` manages settings, members, Kanban, milestones, meetings, KPIs and files. Normal members read project scope and update only tasks assigned to them. A non-restricted file is readable to members; a restricted file additionally requires an explicit user or additive-role permission. Storage repeats the metadata decision through `can_read_project_file`.

Research is visible to Admin, the named supervisor, research members, and — for a public study — every active employee. Admin creates a research record and must name its supervisor; the supervisor or a member labelled `supervisor`/`lead` manages the record, team, datasets, ethics approvals, publications, documents, and research tasks. An ordinary researcher reads the research scope, logs experiments under their own name, updates the experiments they created, and moves only tasks assigned to them; they cannot register datasets, file ethics approvals, record publications, or add teammates. A non-restricted document is readable to members; a restricted document additionally requires an explicit user or additive-role grant, and storage repeats that decision through `can_read_research_document`. An approved or rejected ethics record must name who decided it and when. Suspended accounts lose every membership-derived research permission.
